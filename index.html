04 / IDEAS TO GET STARTED

Student project inspiration.

These are example ideas to help teams brainstorm. Choose a real problem, build a small prototype, and make the idea your own.

IDEA 01
♧

Smart Plant Monitor

Problem: Plants may not get the right amount of water.

Example: Use a soil-moisture sensor and a microcontroller to show when watering is needed.

IDEA 02
♻

Smart Waste Segregation

Problem: Recyclable waste is often mixed with other waste.

Example: Build a demo bin that identifies categories using simple sensors or image classification.

IDEA 03
⌕

Campus Help Chatbot

Problem: New students need quick campus information.

Example: Create a website chatbot for department details, office hours, and campus directions.

IDEA 04
⚡

Energy Usage Dashboard

Problem: People may not notice unnecessary electricity use.

Example: Display sample or sensor-based energy readings in a simple dashboard.

IDEA 05
♧

Student Skill Exchange

Problem: Students may struggle to find peers who can teach a skill.

Example: Build a platform to list skills, request peer-learning sessions, and match students.

IDEA 06
⌖

Accessible Campus Map

Problem: Visitors need clear routes to classrooms and facilities.

Example: Create a mobile-friendly map with building details and accessible route notes.

Quick team example: Pick one campus problem → research who it affects → sketch a solution → build a small demo → test it with classmates → prepare a 2-minute explanation.

Project ideas are suggestions, not guaranteed event categories or prizes. Teams should develop and present their own work.
